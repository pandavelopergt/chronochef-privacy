Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

Overview

ChronoChef is a timer application. We aim to collect as little personal data as possible. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your choices.

Data we collect

  • Local settings: Alarm file paths and basic preferences are stored on your device using Shared Preferences. This data is not sent to our servers.
  • Crash and performance data (platform-specific): If you opt in to platform diagnostics (e.g., Google Play pre-launch), anonymized technical data may be processed by those platforms.

Advertising and third parties

ChronoChef integrates Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, coarse location, and usage data to deliver and measure ads in accordance with Google’s policies. We do not access or store this data ourselves.

For more information, please review:
• Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
• AdMob & Mobile Ads Policies: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Consent and controls

In regions subject to privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR/EEA), you may be presented with a consent dialog to choose personalized or non‑personalized ads. You can update consent choices via the app’s consent prompt (if available) or your device ad settings.

Data retention

Local preferences remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Ad-related data retention is governed by Google and its partners.

Children’s privacy

ChronoChef is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us to request deletion.

Security

We use industry-standard practices provided by the operating system and SDKs to safeguard data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
